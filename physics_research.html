Physics Research

  • Researched for 2 years in a group of 3 how scintillating bubble chambers detect particle interactions between molecules in superheated liquids.
  • Using the HOOMD-Blue Python library, modeled particle interactions by adding hot spikes in the centers of simulated superheated Lennard-Jones fluids. Particle interactions were recorded.
  • Results provided necessary conditions for bubble nucleation and can be used to improve existing models of bubble nucleation that help understand dark matter particle interactions.
