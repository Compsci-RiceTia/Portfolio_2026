Design Thinking & Communication (DTC) Quarter Project

  • Worked in a group of 4 for 10 weeks to assist a client with her line of sight while grocery shopping.
  • Used epoxy and heavy machinery to attach the flexible tripod legs, selfie stick with phone holder, plastic charger holder, and threaded insert together to produce the final product.
  • The project met all project requirements: the device was portable, lightweight, socially acceptable, and allowed Judy to see above her and made the client more independent.
  • Utilized tools like Meeting Minutes, GANTT and RAM charts for organization.
  • Created a 100+ page report documenting our process. Project was presented at expo and given to client.
